# Project Overview 
A sales and customer analytics solution built in Power BI to transform transactional data into actionable business insights. The project incorporates data modeling, Power Query transformations, and advanced DAX measures to analyze sales performance, diagnose performance drivers, perform root cause analysis, and evaluate customer behavior through RFM analysis.

## Home
A home page designed for report navigation, featuring buttons that provide direct access to the different pages of the report.

## Executive Summary
A business intelligence dashboard developed to provide an executive-level view of sales performance. It brings key performance indicators and interactive visualizations together in a single view, supporting performance monitoring and data-driven exploration across key business dimensions.

## Performance Diagnosis 
A diagnostic analysis of sales performance across month-over-month and year-over-year periods. The page decomposes changes in sales into volume and value-related drivers, enabling a structured assessment of how orders, quantity, Average Order Value (AOV), Average Selling Price (ASP), and Average Quantity per Order (AQO) contribute to performance changes.

## Root Cause Analysis
A structured root cause analysis that goes beyond overall performance changes to identify the underlying drivers of AQO and ASP. The analysis separates within-country, country mix, product mix, and price effects, highlighting the specific countries and products contributing to each movement.

## Customer RFM & Behavior Analysis
A customer analytics view that combines lifecycle tracking and RFM segmentation to assess customer engagement, movement, and value. It provides visibility into customer status and segment evolution over time while highlighting high-value at-risk customers for further analysis.

## Buisness Recommendations
A decision-support view that translates analytical findings into actionable business priorities. It focuses on customer retention, customer engagement, product mix, and market-level opportunities to support targeted business actions and sustained performance.
